# ECON1626 Labour Policy Proposal

This repository contains an Australian policy proposal on how government can support workers whose tasks are changing because of artificial intelligence.

## Project files

- [`proposal.md`](proposal.md) — policy analysis and recommendation to the Department of Employment and Workplace Relations.
- [`reflection.md`](reflection.md) — reflection on the use of AI during the assessment.

## Policy topic

**Training Before Displacement: An AI Workforce Transition Pilot for Australia**

The proposal is anchored in the Australian Government's March 2026 response to the *Future of Work: Inquiry into the Digital Transformation of Workplaces*.

## Navigation

Open `proposal.md` first for the policy brief, then read `reflection.md` for the AI-use reflection. References use RMIT Harvard style and include links to the original sources.
